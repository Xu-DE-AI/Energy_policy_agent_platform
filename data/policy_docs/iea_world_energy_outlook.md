# World Energy Outlook — analytical use brief

**Publisher:** International Energy Agency  
**Topic:** scenarios  
**Source:** https://www.iea.org/reports/world-energy-outlook-2024  
**Document type:** Original analytical summary; consult the official source for legal interpretation.

## Executive summary

The IEA World Energy Outlook uses scenarios to explore plausible pathways under different policy, technology, investment and behavioural assumptions. Scenarios are not unconditional predictions. When using them in planning, document the scenario name, publication vintage, geography, time horizon, and key assumptions. Sensitivity analysis should examine how conclusions change under different demand, fuel-price, technology-cost and policy trajectories. Never combine figures from different scenario vintages without documenting the basis.

## Analyst checklist

- Verify the current official text and effective date.
- Separate binding obligations from policy goals, guidance, and scenarios.
- Record geography, entity scope, activity, thresholds, and reporting period.
- Preserve the source URL and exact version used in the decision record.
- Escalate legal interpretation to qualified counsel or compliance owners.
