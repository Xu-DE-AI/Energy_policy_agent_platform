# EU Just Transition — regional planning brief

**Publisher:** European Commission  
**Topic:** just_transition  
**Source:** https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/priorities-2019-2024/european-green-deal/finance-and-green-deal/just-transition-mechanism_en  
**Document type:** Original analytical summary; consult the official source for legal interpretation.

## Executive summary

The Just Transition framework seeks to support regions and workers facing significant socioeconomic impacts from the transition to climate neutrality. Funding eligibility is programme-specific and can depend on territorial plans, eligible activities, calls, co-financing and dates. A project should not assume that a region or technology is automatically eligible. Record the funding instrument, call text, applicant type, eligible cost categories, deadlines and evidence requirements before advising on funding availability.
