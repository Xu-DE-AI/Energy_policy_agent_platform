# Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism — implementation brief

**Publisher:** European Commission  
**Topic:** trade_and_carbon  
**Source:** https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism_en  
**Document type:** Original analytical summary; consult the official source for legal interpretation.

## Executive summary

The Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism addresses the carbon content of selected imported goods and is designed to work alongside the EU ETS. Scope is defined by product categories and customs codes; reporting and financial obligations depend on the applicable phase and rules. A compliance workflow should verify product classification, embedded-emissions methodology, importer role, reporting period and the current transitional or definitive-phase requirements. Do not generalise CBAM obligations to all imports.
