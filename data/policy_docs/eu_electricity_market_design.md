# EU electricity market design — implementation brief

**Publisher:** European Commission  
**Topic:** electricity_markets  
**Source:** https://energy.ec.europa.eu/topics/markets-and-consumers/electricity-market-design_en  
**Document type:** Original analytical summary; consult the official source for legal interpretation.

## Executive summary

EU electricity-market reforms aim to improve consumer protection, support investment in clean generation, and make the market more resilient to price shocks. Market design affects long-term contracts, flexibility, demand response, storage, and the interaction between wholesale and retail prices. Commercial conclusions depend on country-level implementation and contract details. A decision brief should separate wholesale price exposure from retail tariff exposure and identify whether the asset can respond to time-varying prices.
