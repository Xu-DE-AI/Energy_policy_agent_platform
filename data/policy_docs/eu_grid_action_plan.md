# EU Grid Action Plan — planning brief

**Publisher:** European Commission  
**Topic:** grids  
**Source:** https://energy.ec.europa.eu/topics/infrastructure/european-grids_en  
**Document type:** Original analytical summary; consult the official source for legal interpretation.

## Executive summary

Grid capacity, interconnection and permitting can constrain the deployment of renewable generation, storage and electrification. Grid planning therefore requires coordinated assumptions about generation build-out, demand growth, flexibility, and cross-border flows. Project assessments should identify the relevant transmission or distribution operator, connection queue, curtailment risk, and local network constraints. EU-level plans provide strategic context but do not establish the connection capacity or delivery date for a particular project.
