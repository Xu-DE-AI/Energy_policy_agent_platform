# EU Batteries Regulation — implementation brief

**Publisher:** European Commission  
**Topic:** storage  
**Source:** https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/waste-and-recycling/batteries_en  
**Document type:** Original analytical summary; consult the official source for legal interpretation.

## Executive summary

The EU Batteries Regulation introduces lifecycle requirements for batteries, including sustainability, safety, labelling, due diligence and end-of-life management. Requirements differ by battery category and may phase in on different dates. For energy-storage investments, capture battery type, market placement date, responsible economic operator, documentation duties, and applicable delegated or implementing acts. A high-level policy summary is not a substitute for checking the current consolidated regulation and its specific articles.
