# EU Emissions Trading System — implementation brief

**Publisher:** European Commission  
**Topic:** carbon_pricing  
**Source:** https://climate.ec.europa.eu/eu-action/carbon-markets/eu-emissions-trading-system-eu-ets_en  
**Document type:** Original analytical summary; consult the official source for legal interpretation.

## Executive summary

The EU Emissions Trading System places a cap on emissions in covered sectors and uses tradable allowances to create a carbon price signal. Coverage, allocation rules, monitoring and reporting obligations differ by sector and can change over time. Exposure analysis should identify the installation or activity, applicable phase, verified emissions, allowance position, and relevant reporting year. A scenario model should treat carbon prices as uncertain assumptions rather than guaranteed forecasts.

## Analyst checklist

- Verify the current official text and effective date.
